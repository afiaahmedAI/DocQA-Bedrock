Acme Corp Return Policy

Acme Corp accepts returns within 30 days of purchase for a full refund, provided the item is unused and in its original packaging. To start a return, customers should visit acme.example.com/returns and enter their order number and email address.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after Acme Corp receives the returned item. Shipping costs for the original order are non-refundable unless the return is due to a defect or shipping error on Acme Corp's part.

Items marked as "Final Sale" cannot be returned or exchanged. Gift cards are also non-refundable.

For defective items, customers can request a free replacement instead of a refund. Replacement requests are processed within 2 business days of the defect being confirmed by Acme Corp's support team.

Customers outside the United States are responsible for return shipping costs, except in cases of confirmed product defects. International returns typically take 10-15 business days to process due to customs handling.

If a package is lost in transit during the return process, customers should contact Acme Corp support with their tracking number, and a replacement or refund will be issued after the carrier confirms the loss.
