Acme Corp Product FAQ - Acme SmartKettle 2.0

What is the warranty period for the Acme SmartKettle 2.0?
The SmartKettle 2.0 comes with a 2-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects. The warranty does not cover damage from drops, misuse, or unauthorized repairs.

How do I pair the SmartKettle 2.0 with the Acme app?
Hold the power button for 5 seconds until the LED ring flashes blue, then open the Acme app, go to Settings > Add Device, and select "SmartKettle 2.0" from the list. Pairing typically takes under 30 seconds.

What water capacity does the SmartKettle 2.0 support?
The kettle holds a maximum of 1.7 liters and a minimum fill line of 0.5 liters. Boiling below the minimum fill line may trigger the auto shut-off safety feature.

Can I use the SmartKettle 2.0 without the app?
Yes. The kettle has physical buttons for power and temperature presets (Green Tea, Black Tea, Coffee, Boil) that work independently of the app or Wi-Fi connection.

Why is my kettle not connecting to Wi-Fi?
The SmartKettle 2.0 only supports 2.4GHz Wi-Fi networks, not 5GHz. Ensure your router broadcasts a 2.4GHz band and that the kettle is within 10 meters of the router during setup.

Is the SmartKettle 2.0 dishwasher safe?
The kettle base with electronics is not dishwasher safe and should never be submerged in water. The removable water reservoir and lid are dishwasher safe on the top rack only.
